Algorithm Principle

💡 FCFS (First Come First Serve)

In FCFS, processes are executed in the order of their arrival. The process that arrives first is given the CPU first and runs until completion.

Formula Cheat Sheet

Completion Time (CT) Time at which process finishes
Turnaround Time (TAT) CT − AT
Waiting Time (WT) TAT − BT
Response Time (RT) ST − AT
Start Time (ST) First time process gets CPU
Burst Time (BT) Total CPU execution time
